# Use your projects inside a page

A website or page can show your other projects: a form, an interactive demo, a banner, an FAQ, a menu or a tour. You build each one once, and it can appear in many places.

## Add one

1. Open your website or page in the editor.
2. Open **Add**, then **Projects**.
3. Click a project. It's added after the selected block, and you can drag it anywhere.

You can also add the **Your project** block from **Blocks** and choose the project in its settings.

## Publish both

Visitors only see a project that's published. If it isn't, the editor tells you, and that spot stays empty on your live page.

To change it later, edit and publish that project. Your page picks up the change by itself, so it doesn't need publishing again.

## How each kind behaves

- **Forms, demos, FAQs and menus** appear where you put them.
- **Banners** that are bars appear where you put them, rather than at the top of the window.
- **Popups and tours** open over the page for visitors. In the editor they show as a short note, so they don't cover your work.

Answers to a form arrive under that form's **Responses**, and its activity is counted under that project in **Analytics**.
