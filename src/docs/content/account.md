# Your account

## Profile and time zone

Click your name at the bottom of the left menu. There you can change your name, job title and password.

**Time zone** sets how dates appear across the app, such as when a project was updated or a response arrived. If you don't choose one, the app uses your device's. Analytics are still counted per day in UTC, so the same day means the same thing for everyone in your team.

## Plans

Open **Plans** in the left menu to see what your workspace is using, and what each plan includes.

Limits can apply to projects overall, to team members, and to each kind of project. When you reach one, your existing projects keep working. You just can't add more until you upgrade or remove some.

Online payment for plans isn't available yet. The workspace owner chooses **Ask for this plan**, and we set it up with you and switch the workspace by hand.

## Support

Open **Support** in the left menu and tell us what you were trying to do. We reply on that same page. Email replies aren't available yet, so check back there for an answer.

Our replies are in your request's conversation, and you can reply to them until the request is closed. After that, please start a new one.

## Messages from us

Now and then we may show a message at the top of the app, such as a tip or news about your plan. Close it when you've read it.

## Tours

The first time you open the dashboard and the editor, a short tour shows you around. To see one again, click **Take a tour** on the dashboard, or the question mark at the bottom of the editor's left menu.
